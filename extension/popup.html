Blog Automation Studio

ChatGPT tabs open by themselves for queued articles (up to Task Concurrency) and close when an article's ChatGPT part is done.

Keep the ChatGPT window open and not minimized; the extension switches between its tabs by itself. If ChatGPT asks you to log in or to verify you are human, do it in that window — the article continues by itself.

    After a reload the ChatGPT tabs open again by themselves within 30 seconds when articles are waiting.
